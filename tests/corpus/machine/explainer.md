# Why Evals Matter

Evaluations play a key role in modern AI development. Here's the thing: most teams get this wrong.

Studies show that robust evaluation significantly improves outcomes. It's worth noting that the data tells us everything we need to know. The key insight is that evals are the new unit tests.

So what does this mean? It means we must leverage comprehensive and meticulous testing. Despite these challenges, the field continues to grow.

In conclusion, evals are crucial. They really are.
